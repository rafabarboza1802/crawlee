# Best Construction: property managers, HOAs and facilities

## Who we are
Best Construction, Pinole, CA (bestconstruction.pro). 19+ years in the Bay Area, licensed and insured,
BBB-accredited. Service area: about 100 miles around Pinole (SF Bay Area and surrounding counties).

## What we sell to this segment (and nothing else)
- Commercial pavers: walkways, courtyards, entries, parking and drive areas.
- Pool deck pavers: replacing cracked or slippery concrete pool decks.
- Build and remodel work on the property.

Do NOT pitch to this segment: backyard packages for new homes, the Outdoor Living Partner Program,
demolition/excavation sub work, or anything priced for home builders.

## Why property managers and boards buy from us
- Line-item bids: easy for a board or owner to review, compare and approve.
- Photo-documented jobs: before, during and after photos for the property's records.
- We work around tenants and residents: phased areas, access kept open, quiet hours respected.
- One property walk, one fixed number. No change-order surprises.

## Good reasons to reach out (use only what the prospect's own site or public record shows)
- They manage apartment communities, HOAs, office parks or retail centers with walkways, courtyards or pools.
- They are hiring maintenance or capital-projects staff.
- Capital budget season (fall) or a reserve study coming up.

## The ask
A 30-minute property walk, then a written line-item number. Rafa does the walk.

## Facts to never invent
No made-up past clients, project names, square footages, prices for their property or timelines.
If we don't know it, ask.
