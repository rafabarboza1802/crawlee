# Best Construction: Outdoor Living Partner Program for home builders

## Who we are
Best Construction, Pinole, CA (bestconstruction.pro). 19+ years in the Bay Area, licensed and insured,
BBB-accredited. Service area: about 100 miles around Pinole (SF Bay Area and surrounding counties).

## What we sell to this segment (and nothing else)
The Outdoor Living Partner Program: fixed-price backyard packages the builder sells as design-studio options.

| Tier | Price to buyer | Builder margin |
|---|---|---|
| Backyard Foundation | $12K | $3.5K |
| Outdoor Living | $32K | $10K |
| Ultimate Oasis | $62K | $20K |

Do NOT pitch to this segment: commercial pavers, pool decks for HOAs, demolition or excavation sub work.

## Why builders buy
- New margin on every home sold, with no extra work for the builder's own crews.
- Finished-yard model homes sell homes; bare dirt doesn't.
- About $320K of builder margin per 100 homes at a 40% attach rate.
- Zero-risk 90-day pilot: we build one model backyard at cost, and the builder measures the attach rate.

## Good reasons to reach out (use only what the prospect's own site or public record shows)
- A new community or phase opening, model homes under construction, a design studio.
- Hiring design-studio or new-home sales staff.

A 20-minute call with Rafa about running the 90-day pilot at one community. If they ask for details, offer the
8-slide program overview (Rafa sends it by hand; Harvey does not attach files).

No made-up builder partners, attach rates already achieved, or community names. If we don't know it, ask.
